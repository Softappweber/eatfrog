Strategy #16

Practice Creative Procrastination

You can't do everything, so deliberately choose what you will NOT do.

"Procrastinate on purpose. Decide now what you will never do again."

The Breakdown

Everyone procrastinates. The question is: what are you procrastinating on? Creative procrastination means making a conscious decision to ignore low-value tasks. Put those tasks at the bottom of your list and refuse to feel guilty about them.

Why It Works

  • Protects your time. You stop letting trivial tasks eat your day.
  • Removes guilt. You give yourself permission to say no.
  • Prioritizes impact. You force yourself to focus only on high-value work.
Real World Example

A freelancer decides to creatively procrastinate on organizing their email folders, designing a new logo for themselves, and attending every free webinar. They know that writing proposals and delivering client work is what pays the bills. Everything else can wait indefinitely.
