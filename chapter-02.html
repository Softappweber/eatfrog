Strategy #2

Plan Every Day in Advance

Think on paper. Every minute spent planning saves five minutes in execution.

"Planning is bringing the future into the present so you can do something about it now."

The Breakdown

The most productive people don't wake up wondering what to do. They already know. The night before, or first thing in the morning, they sit down and write out a list of everything they need to accomplish. Then they organize that list by priority.

Why It Works

  • Eliminates decision fatigue. You don't waste mental energy choosing what to do first.
  • Creates momentum. Starting your day with a clear plan gives you a psychological head start.
  • Increases accuracy. When you plan on paper, you see conflicts and bottlenecks before they happen.
Real World Example

A freelancer who ends their day by writing tomorrow's task list sleeps peacefully. They wake up, look at the list, and immediately start on task #1. Compare this to a freelancer who wakes up, opens their laptop, and spends 30 minutes just trying to figure out where to begin.
