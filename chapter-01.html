Strategy #1

Set the Table

Decide exactly what you want. Clarity is not a luxury—it is the price of entry.

"If you don't know where you are going, any road will take you there."

The Brutal Truth

Most people never decide what they actually want. They have wishes, not goals. They say "I want more money" or "I want to be successful." That is not clarity. That is fog.

Successful people do something different. They sit down with a blank piece of paper and write down exactly what they want. They define the amount, the deadline, and the measurement.

Rule: A goal that is not written down is just a dream. A dream has no power.

Why This Works

  • Clarity activates your brain. When you define a target, your mind automatically starts scanning for ways to hit it.
  • Writing creates commitment. A written goal is a contract with yourself. It becomes real.
  • Specificity kills procrastination. "Lose weight" is easy to avoid. "Lose 5 kg by March 1st" demands action.
  • Measurement drives focus. What gets measured gets managed. What gets managed gets done.
Real World Example

The Vague Employee: Priya works in marketing. She tells herself she wants a promotion. She works hard but never writes down what "promotion" means. Three years pass. No promotion.

The Clear Employee: Priya sits down and writes: "I will be promoted to Senior Marketing Manager by December 31st. I will complete 3 major campaigns, generate 50 qualified leads, and manage a team of 2 interns." Now she has a table. Every day she knows exactly what to work on. She gets the promotion in 10 months.

Same person. Same company. The only difference was clarity.
