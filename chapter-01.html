Strategy #1

Set the Table

Decide exactly what you want. Clarity is the starting point of all achievement.

"If you don't know where you are going, any road will take you there."

The Breakdown

Before you can accomplish anything, you must first define exactly what "it" is. Most people drift through their days reacting to whatever appears in front of them. Setting the table means sitting down with a blank piece of paper and deciding, in very specific terms, what you want your life, your career, and your day to look like.

Why It Works

  • Clarity removes confusion. When your goal is crystal clear, your brain automatically starts looking for solutions.
  • Written goals activate your reticular activating system. You start noticing opportunities and resources that were always there but invisible to you.
  • Specificity beats vagueness. "I want to be rich" is a wish. "I want to earn $10,000/month by December 1st" is a plan.
Real World Example

Imagine a salesperson who wakes up and thinks, "I need to sell more." They spend the day answering emails, chatting with coworkers, and feeling busy but making no progress. Now imagine a salesperson who wakes up and reads their written goal: "I will make 20 cold calls before 10 AM to close 2 new deals this week." By 10 AM, they are already ahead of everyone else.
