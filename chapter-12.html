Strategy #12

Take It One Oil Barrel at a Time

You can accomplish any task if you just take it one step at a time.

"By the yard it's hard, but inch by inch, anything's a cinch."

The Breakdown

Sometimes the frog is so big it feels impossible. Looking at the whole project overwhelms you. The secret is to focus only on the very next step. Do not look at the mountain; just look at your feet. Take one small action, then the next, and soon you are halfway there.

Why It Works

  • Reduces overwhelm. You shrink the task down to something manageable.
  • Builds momentum. Starting is the hardest part. Once you start, you keep going.
  • Creates hope. Every small step proves you are making progress.
Real World Example

A freelancer needs to build a 20-page website. It feels massive. Instead of thinking about all 20 pages, they think: "I will just open the design file and build the navigation bar." Once the nav bar is done, they naturally move to the hero section, and so on.
