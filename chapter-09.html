Strategy #9

Do Your Homework

Learn what you need to learn so you can do the job right the first time.

"The more you know, the less you fear."

The Breakdown

Often we procrastinate because we feel incompetent or unsure about how to do a task. The solution is to become a student. Research, watch tutorials, read articles, or ask an expert. The more knowledge you have, the more confident and faster you become.

Why It Works

  • Reduces anxiety. Fear of the unknown disappears when you know how to proceed.
  • Improves quality. You make fewer mistakes and produce better results.
  • Speeds up execution. You stop second-guessing yourself.
Real World Example

A freelancer is asked to set up an online store. They have never done it. Instead of panicking, they spend one hour watching a YouTube tutorial and reading a guide. Now they start the project with a clear roadmap instead of clicking buttons randomly.

Your Turn

What is one task you have been avoiding because you don't know how to do it? What research can you do today to close the knowledge gap?
