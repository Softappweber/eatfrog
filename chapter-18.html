Strategy #18

Slice and Dice the Task

Break large tasks into small, manageable pieces.

"How do you eat an elephant? One bite at a time."

The Breakdown

A massive project can feel like standing at the base of Mount Everest. The peak is invisible, and the climb seems impossible. The solution is to "slice and dice." Break the project down into tiny, specific, non-intimidating steps. Instead of writing "Build website," you write "Open editor, create header file, write 5 lines of code." Once the first slice is done, the next slice feels easier.

Why It Works

  • Makes starting easy. A tiny step is less frightening than a giant leap.
  • Provides a roadmap. You see exactly what to do next.
  • Builds confidence. Every small completion releases a bit of dopamine, pushing you forward.
Real World Example

An artist wants to paint a large mural. The blank wall terrifies them. So they slice it: Day 1, sketch the outline in pencil. Day 2, paint the background. Day 3, paint the main subject. By breaking it down, the artist only ever has to focus on one small section. Before they know it, the mural is done.
