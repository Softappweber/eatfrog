Strategy #3

Apply the 80/20 Rule

20% of your activities will account for 80% of your results.

"The vital few are worth more than the trivial many."

The Breakdown

Vilfredo Pareto noticed that in society, 20% of the people owned 80% of the land. This principle applies everywhere. In your work, only a small fraction of your tasks truly move the needle. Your job is to identify that vital 20% and focus on it relentlessly.

Why It Works

  • Maximizes impact. You get the most reward for the least amount of effort.
  • Prevents busywork. You stop doing tasks that feel urgent but are actually unimportant.
  • Clarifies value. You start asking, "Is this task in my top 20% or my bottom 80%?"
Real World Example

A freelancer has 10 tasks on their list. Two of those tasks—finishing a project for a high-paying client and sending a proposal to a potential retainer client—will bring in 80% of their income this month. The other 8 tasks (checking email, organizing files) will only bring in 20%. Eat the 2 frogs first.

Your Turn

Look at your to-do list. Which TWO tasks, if completed today, would generate the most significant results?
