Strategy #13

Put the Pressure on Yourself

Don't wait for the world to push you. Push yourself.

"The best way to escape pressure is to create it yourself."

The Breakdown

Most people wait for a deadline, a boss, or a crisis to force them into action. High performers create their own deadlines. They pretend they have only half the time they actually have. By manufacturing urgency, they tap into deep focus and get more done.

Why It Works

  • Eliminates laziness. You stop waiting for the "perfect time" to start.
  • Sharpens focus. Self-imposed deadlines stop you from wasting time.
  • Builds confidence. Every time you hit your own deadline, you trust yourself more.
Real World Example

A freelancer has 5 days to deliver a project. Instead of relaxing for 4 days, they tell themselves, "I will deliver this in 48 hours." They work with intense focus. They deliver early, the client is amazed, and they free up 3 days for new work or rest.

Your Turn

What is one task you are currently doing slowly? What new, earlier deadline will you set for yourself to force faster action?
