Strategy #10

Leverage Your Special Talents

Focus on what you do best, and delegate or eliminate the rest.

"You are designed for a unique purpose. Work within your genius."

The Breakdown

You have specific strengths—things you do easily that others find difficult. When you work in your area of genius, you produce more in less time and enjoy the process. When you force yourself to do tasks you hate, you procrastinate and deliver poor work.

Why It Works

  • Increases flow. You enter a state of deep focus when doing what you're good at.
  • Boosts energy. Working on strengths energizes you rather than draining you.
  • Maximizes income. You get paid more for skills where you are excellent.
Real World Example

A freelance developer is excellent at coding but terrible at customer support. They spend 10 hours a week answering basic client emails (hating every minute). If they hire a virtual assistant for a small fee to handle those emails, they free up 10 hours to code, which earns them 5x more money.

Your Turn

What is your single greatest talent or skill? What tasks are you doing right now that are outside this talent and could be delegated?
