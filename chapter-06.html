Strategy #6

Focus on Key Result Areas

Identify the specific skills that determine your success, and master them.

"Your weakest key skill sets the height of your income and success."

The Breakdown

Every job has 5 to 7 key result areas (KRAs). These are the core skills you must do well to succeed. For a freelancer, KRAs might be: finding clients, delivering quality work, managing time, communicating, and invoicing. You are only as strong as your weakest KRA.

Why It Works

  • Identifies bottlenecks. You see exactly where you are struggling.
  • Targets improvement. You stop practicing what you're already good at and fix what's broken.
  • Improves confidence. Mastering a weak area removes fear and procrastination.
Real World Example

A freelancer is brilliant at design but terrible at sending invoices. Because they delay invoicing, they always have cash flow problems. If they spend one day fixing their invoicing system, they solve 50% of their financial stress.

Your Turn

What is your weakest Key Result Area right now? What one action can you take this week to improve it?
