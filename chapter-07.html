Strategy #7

Obey the Law of Forced Efficiency

There is never enough time to do everything, but there is always enough time to do the most important thing.

"You can't manage time, but you can manage yourself."

The Breakdown

The reality is brutal: you will never catch up. You will never finish every email, every small task, or every request. High performers accept this. They force themselves to focus only on the tasks that truly matter and let the rest go.

Why It Works

  • Removes guilt. You stop feeling bad about not doing everything.
  • Sharpens focus. Since you can't do it all, you naturally choose the best thing.
  • Reduces stress. You accept that "good enough" on the important things beats perfection on the trivial.
Real World Example

A freelancer has 50 unread emails, 10 small admin tasks, and one major client project due. They obey the law by ignoring the emails and admin tasks until the major project is shipped. The client is happy, and the freelancer survives another day.

Your Turn

Look at your current to-do list. What is the ONE task that, if you ignore everything else, will make the biggest difference?
