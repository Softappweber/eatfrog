Strategy #5

Practice the ABCDE Method

Organize your list by priority before you start working.

"The first law of success is concentration: to bend all energies to one point."

The Breakdown

Take your to-do list and assign a letter to each task. "A" tasks are must-do (major consequences). "B" tasks are should-do (minor consequences). "C" tasks are nice-to-do (no consequences). "D" tasks are delegate. "E" tasks are eliminate. Always work on your A-1 task before anything else.

Why It Works

  • Forces evaluation. You must actively decide what is truly important.
  • Prevents overwhelm. You know exactly what to do next.
  • Boosts efficiency. You never waste time on "C" tasks when "A" tasks are waiting.
Real World Example

A freelancer lists: write proposal (A-1), answer client email (B), organize Dropbox (C), post on social media (D—delegate or delete). If they spend two hours organizing Dropbox, they avoided the one task that would actually bring in money.

Your Turn

Write down your top 5 tasks for tomorrow. Label them A, B, C, D, or E. What is your A-1 task?
