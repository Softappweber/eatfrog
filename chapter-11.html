Strategy #11

Identify Your Key Constraints

Find the bottleneck that is slowing you down, and remove it.

"The chain is only as strong as its weakest link."

The Breakdown

Between you and your goal, there is always a limiting factor—a constraint. It might be a lack of money, a lack of time, a lack of knowledge, or a specific person. Your job is to identify exactly what is holding you back and attack that constraint directly.

Why It Works

  • Clarifies the battleground. You stop fighting symptoms and fix the root cause.
  • Saves time. You don't waste effort on areas that aren't the problem.
  • Produces rapid results. Removing one major blockage creates massive momentum.
Real World Example

A freelancer wants to earn more money. Their immediate thought is "I need more clients." But upon reflection, they realize their real constraint is that their portfolio is outdated, so clients don't trust them. They spend two days fixing their portfolio. Suddenly, clients say yes.

Your Turn

What is your biggest goal right now? What is the ONE major constraint standing between you and that goal?
