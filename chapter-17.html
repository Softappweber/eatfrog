Strategy #17

Do the Most Difficult Task First

The task you fear the most is almost always the one you must do first.

"The hardest part of any important task is getting started on it in the first place."

The Breakdown

Every day, one task stands out as the most challenging. It is the one that makes your stomach tighten when you think about it. This is your biggest, ugliest frog. Human nature is to avoid it and do easy tasks to feel busy. But true productivity means attacking the hardest task when your energy and willpower are at their peak—usually first thing in the morning.

Why It Works

  • Willpower is finite. You have the most mental energy at the start of the day. Use it on the hard stuff.
  • Relieves anxiety. The dread of the task hangs over you all day. Doing it first removes that weight.
  • Creates momentum. Once the worst is over, every other task feels like a walk in the park.
Real World Example

A student has three assignments and one massive chemistry exam to study for. They hate chemistry. If they study chemistry first thing in the morning, the rest of the day feels easy. If they leave it until evening, they will procrastinate all day, dreading it. By 9 PM, they are tired, stressed, and study poorly. The task didn't change—the timing did.

Your Turn

What is the single most difficult task you are facing right now? Commit to doing it tomorrow before 10:00 AM.
