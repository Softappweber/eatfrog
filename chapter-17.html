Strategy #17

Do the Most Difficult Task First

The task you fear the most is almost always the one you must do first.

"The hardest part of any important task is getting started on it in the first place."

The Brutal Truth

Every day, one task stands out as the most challenging. It is the one that makes your stomach tighten when you think about it. This is your ugliest frog.

Human nature is to avoid it. You do easy tasks to feel busy. You check email. You organize files. You make coffee. But the whole time, the ugly frog sits in the back of your mind, draining your energy.

High performers do the opposite. They attack the hardest task first, when their willpower is strongest. They eat the ugliest frog before they do anything else.

Rule: The first hour of your day determines the rest of it. Spend it on the hardest thing.

Why This Works

  • Willpower is finite. You have the most mental energy at the start of the day. Use it on the hard stuff.
  • It relieves anxiety. The dread of the task hangs over you all day. Doing it first removes that weight.
  • It creates momentum. Once the worst is over, every other task feels like a walk in the park.
  • It builds discipline. You train yourself to face discomfort instead of running from it.
Real World Example

The Lawyer: Priya is a junior lawyer. She has to make a difficult phone call to a client whose case is not going well. She has been dreading it for days. She wakes up thinking about it. She goes to bed thinking about it. The call takes 15 minutes, but she has spent 10 hours worrying about it.

Then Priya changes her approach. She makes the call first thing in the morning, before checking email, before anything else. The call is uncomfortable, but it is over in 15 minutes. The rest of her day feels light. She is free.

The task was never the problem. The anticipation was the problem.
