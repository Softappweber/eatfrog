Strategy #4

Consider the Consequences

Your ability to think through the future determines your success today.

"Long-term thinking improves short-term decision making."

The Breakdown

Every action you take has a consequence. Superior thinkers constantly project forward and ask: "If I do this task right now, what will be the outcome? If I don't do it, what will be the cost?" The clearer you are about the future impact, the easier it is to identify your biggest frog.

Why It Works

  • Creates urgency. When you realize a task will save you time or money later, you do it now.
  • Reduces procrastination. You see the pain of not doing it.
  • Aligns with goals. You naturally gravitate toward tasks that have the biggest positive ripple effect.
Real World Example

A freelancer has a choice: watch TV or learn a new skill (like video editing). Watching TV has zero future benefit. Learning video editing could double their income in six months. The consequence of learning is massive; the consequence of watching TV is zero.

Your Turn

What is one task you have been avoiding that will have the biggest positive impact on your future if you complete it this week?
