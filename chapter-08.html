Strategy #8

Prepare Thoroughly Before You Begin

Have everything you need at hand before you sit down to work.

"Preparation prevents poor performance."

The Breakdown

Before you start eating your frog, set up your workspace. Gather all the files, passwords, links, tools, and resources you need. If you have to stand up to find something, you break your flow and give procrastination an opening.

Why It Works

  • Eliminates friction. You remove physical and digital barriers to starting.
  • Builds momentum. A clean desk and a clear mind make it easy to dive in.
  • Signals commitment. Preparing tells your brain, "We are doing this now."
Real World Example

A writer sits down to write a blog post. They spend 15 minutes hunting for their notes, checking their phone, and adjusting their chair. By the time they start typing, they are drained. A prepared writer opens their laptop, notes already on the screen, phone in another room. They type immediately.
